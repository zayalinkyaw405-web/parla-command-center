## 1. National Macro Breakdown (2026 Overview)

Five years following the February 2021 military coup d'état, Myanmar's territorial sovereign authority has collapsed into a complex, decentralized **mosaic of non-state governance**:
- **Resistance & EAO Controlled Territory:** **42% to 55%** of national landmass (predominantly ethnic peripheral borderlands, mountain ranges, and critical trade corridors).
- **SAC Junta Military Controlled Territory:** **21% to 33%** (strictly confined to the central lowland corridor, Naypyidaw, Yangon, Mandalay, and major garrison urban enclaves).
- **Actively Contested & Transition Zones:** **20% to 37%** (shifting rural frontlines, active artillery/airstrike corridors, and mixed PDF-military operational sectors).

## 3. Regional Breakdown & Control Analytics (2026)

### A. Rakhine State & Paletwa (Western Theater)
- **Dominant Actor:** Arakan Army (AA)
- **Control Percentage:** ~92.6% of state territory.
- **Key Holdings:** Kyauktaw, Mrauk-U, Minbya, Ponnagyun, Rathedaung, Buthidaung, Maungdaw, Ramree, Paletwa.
- **SAC Junta Enclaves:** Restricted strictly to the state capital (Sittwe) and portions of the deep-sea port area in Kyaukphyu.

### B. Kachin State (Northern Theater)
- **Dominant Actor:** Kachin Independence Army (KIA)
- **Control Percentage:** ~72.5% of state territory.
- **Key Holdings:** Sumprabum, Lweje border crossing, rare-earth mining hubs in Sadung/Pangwa, Bhamo surrounding corridors.
- **SAC Junta Enclaves:** State capital Myitkyina, Waingmaw outposts, and airport logistics perimeters.

### C. Northern Shan State (Northeastern Borderland)
- **Dominant Actors:** MNDAA, TNLA, UWSA, SSPP
- **Control Percentage:** ~75.0% total non-state control.
- **Key Holdings:** Laukkai (MNDAA), Chinshwehaw, Kunlong, Kyaukme, Hsipaw, Kutkai. Wa Region 2 (UWSA) remains 100% autonomous.
- **Geopolitical Pressure:** China-mediated talks forced partial tactical adjustments around Lashio in 2025, but structural non-state administrative dominance persists.

### D. Chin State (Western Borderland)
- **Dominant Actors:** Chinland Council / CNF & Chin Brotherhood
- **Control Percentage:** ~82.5% of state territory.
- **Key Holdings:** Hakha outskirts, Mindat, Kanpetlet, Matupi, Tedim. Over 90% of military bases in Chin State were overran by 2025–2026.

### E. Kayah (Karenni) State (Southeastern Theater)
- **Dominant Actors:** KNDF, KNPP, Karenni National People's Liberation Front
- **Control Percentage:** ~82.0% of state territory.
- **Key Holdings:** Demoso, Bawlake, Shadaw, Mese, and over 70% of urban perimeter around state capital Loikaw.

### F. Kayin (Karen) State (Southeastern Borderland)
- **Dominant Actor:** KNU / KNLA & Allied Resistance Forces
- **Control Percentage:** ~65.0% of state territory.
- **Key Holdings:** KNU Brigades 1 through 7 operational zones, rural Myawaddy trade access, Dupalaya District.
