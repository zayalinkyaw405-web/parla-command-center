## 🏛️ 2023–2025 Operational Theaters & Nodes Specification

### 1. `THEATER-SHAN-1027`: Northern Shan Theater (Operation 1027 Phases 1 & 2)
- **Primary Force:** **Chaos ➔ Harmony**
- **Actors:** Three Brotherhood Alliance (MNDAA, TNLA, AA) vs. SAC Northeast Regional Military Command (RMC).
- **Core Milestones:**
  - *Phase 1 (Oct 27, 2023 – Jan 2024):* Seizure of Chinshwehaw, Mongko, Hsenwi, Kyukok; surrender of Laukkai garrison (~2,300 troops, 6 Brigadier Generals).
  - *Phase 2 (Jun 25 – Aug 2024):* Seizure of Nawnghkio, Kyaukme, Mogok ruby center; historic fall of Lashio RMC HQ (Aug 3, 2024).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Severe cluster air raids, electronic countermeasures, Chinese border trade gateway shutdown.

### 2. `THEATER-RAKHINE-AA`: Western Maritime & Border Theater
- **Primary Force:** **Yin ➔ Yang**
- **Actors:** Arakan Army (AA) vs. SAC Western Command.
- **Core Milestones:**
  - Overran Paletwa inland port (Jan 2024), Kyauktaw, Mrauk-U, Minbya, Myebon, Buthidaung (May 2024), Thandwe / Ngapali Airport (Jul 2024), Maungdaw border corridor.
  - Besieged Western Command Headquarters at Ann (Q4 2024 – 2025).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Riverine naval artillery, coastal UAV reconnaissance, coastal displacement flows to Bangladesh border.

### 3. `THEATER-KARENNI-1111`: Eastern Mountain Theater (Operations 1107 & 1111)
- **Primary Force:** **Yin ➔ Harmony**
- **Actors:** Karenni Nationalities Defence Force (KNDF), KNPP/KA, allied Southern PDFs vs. SAC Regional Operations Command.
- **Core Milestones:**
  - Urban siege of Loikaw, capture of Loikaw University, Demoso, Bawlake, Mese border crossings, Mawchi mineral mines.
  - Establishment of Karenni Interim Executive Council (IEC) governance across >85% of state.
- **Telemetry Indicators:** High-frequency close-air-support (Mi-35, K-8W), artillery cratering, Starlink mesh networking.

### 4. `THEATER-KACHIN-0307`: Northern Frontier & Jade/Rare-Earth Theater
- **Primary Force:** **Yang**
- **Actors:** Kachin Independence Army (KIA), Kachin PDF vs. SAC Northern Command.
- **Core Milestones:**
  - Operation 0307 (Mar 2024): Overran mountain garrisons around Laiza HQ, Lweje border port, Sumprabum, Injangyang.
  - Seizure of Pangwa and Sadung heavy rare-earth extraction clusters (Oct 2024).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** High-altitude bombing, artillery interdiction of China border routes.

### 5. `THEATER-KAYIN-SOUTH`: Southeastern Trade & Riverine Corridor
- **Primary Force:** **Chaos ➔ Yang**
- **Actors:** KNU/KNLA Brigades 1–7, KNDO, Ramonnya Mon Army (RMA) vs. SAC Coastal & Southeastern Commands.
- **Core Milestones:**
  - Asian Highway 1 interdiction; battle of Myawaddy (Battalion 275) April 2024; RMA consolidation across Ye-Mudon-Mawlamyine axis (2024–2025).
- **Telemetry Indicators:** Cross-border artillery strikes into Thai airspace perimeter, drone bomb drops.

## 🔐 Zero-Trust Ingress & Storage Invariants

All data collected from the 2023–2025 period MUST pass through Parla's non-negotiable security pipeline:
1. **Tier 1 Regex Interception:** Mask email, IPv4/IPv6, MAC addresses, Burmese phone numbers, and citizen ID numbers.
2. **Tier 2 Named Entity Recognition (NER):** Scrub sensitive civilian names and precise target facilities.
3. **Spatial Coarsening:** Coarsen raw latitude/longitude to two decimal places (~1.1 km) or abstract to SHA-256 hashed regional codes (`MYANMAR_REGION_[SECTOR]`).
4. **Merkle Block Envelope:**
   - Sequential monotonic block numbering (`seq_id`).
   - Cryptographic chaining (`prev_hash`).
   - Payload integrity hash (`payload_hash`).
   - HMAC-SHA256 node secret signature (`signature`).
   - Storage in WAL-mode SQLite database (`Data/parla_ledger.db`) under domain `eao_conflict_intel`.

## 🕸️ 100X Inter-Factional Relational Network & Coalitions

All EAOs are modeled in an **N×N relational graph** tracking bilateral affinity, arms transfers, joint battle records, and active territorial flashpoints:

### 1. Major Multilateral Coalitions
- **Three Brotherhood Alliance (3BA):** MNDAA, TNLA, AA (`Cohesion: VERY_HIGH`). Strategic mutual defense, synchronized urban sieges, joint drone command.
- **FPNCC (Northern Hegemony):** UWSA (Chair & Arms Producer), MNDAA, TNLA, AA, KIA, SSPP, NDAA Mong La. Non-aggression with junta, arms production, and border security dialogue.
- **K7 Revolutionary Alliance:** KIA, KNU/KNLA, KNDF/KNPP, CNF/CNA, NUG Ministry of Defence. Nationwide multi-front offensive, arms distribution to Bamar resistance youth.
- **Chin Brotherhood (CB):** CDF-Mindat, Kanpetlet, Matupi, Maraland, ZFU + AA. Alliance operating in southern Chin, counterbalancing Chinland Council.
- **SCEF (Southern & Eastern Council):** KNU, KNPP, CNF, RMA (Ramonnya Mon Army). Federal democratic coordination.

### 2. Strategic Friction Points & Flashpoints
- **TNLA vs. SSPP:** Boundary disputes and overlapping administrative authority across liberated Northern Shan townships (Hsipaw, Kyaukme, Namkham).
- **SSPP vs. RCSS:** Historical inter-Shan fratricidal war over southern corridors and Loi Tai Leng frontier.
- **Chinland Council (CNA) vs. Chin Brotherhood (CB/AA):** Administrative legitimacy dispute over Matupi and Chin governance frameworks.
- **KIA vs. SNA (Shanni):** Territorial contestation in Homalin and Mohnyin; local non-aggression vs. proxy alignment.
- **PNLA vs. PNA:** Pa-O civil war between anti-junta PNLA and regime-backed PNA militia.
- **KNU vs. KNA/BGF:** Saw Chit Thu's pragmatic neutrality around Shwe Kokko and Myawaddy border casinos.
- **AA vs. ARSA/ARA:** Border security, communal tension, and regime instrumentalization of armed Rohingya factions in Buthidaung/Maungdaw.
