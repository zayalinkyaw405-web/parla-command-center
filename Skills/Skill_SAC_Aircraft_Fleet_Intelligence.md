## 📡 3. Acoustic, Doppler & Sensor Signatures for Edge Early Warning

```
Acoustic Detection Spectrum:
0 Hz ------- [18-23 Hz: Helicopter Blade Slap] ------- [40-120 Hz: Su-30 Exhaust Rumble] ------- [300-850 Hz: FTC-2000G Turbojet] ------- [1.2-8.0 kHz: Turbofan Compressor Whine]
```

### Doppler Shift & Speed Modeling:
For a jet aircraft traveling at velocity $v$ toward an acoustic ground sensor:
$$f_{observed} = f_{source} \left( \frac{c}{c - v \cos\theta} \right)$$
where $c \approx 343 \text{ m/s}$ (speed of sound) and $\theta$ is the angle between the aircraft trajectory and the sensor.
- At Mach 0.8 ($v \approx 274 \text{ m/s}$), incoming frequency shifts upward by **up to 4.97×**, producing an unmistakable micro-Doppler compression pattern on tinyML spectrogram classifiers.
- Pre-impact warning windows:
  - **Su-30 / FTC-2000G (Transonic Dive):** 45–75 seconds.
  - **Yak-130 / K-8 (Cruising Approach):** 75–150 seconds.
  - **Mi-35 / Mi-17 (Rotary Ingress):** 3.5–7.0 minutes.

## 📉 4. Airframe Attrition & Logistics Vulnerabilities

### Supply Chain Bottlenecks:
1. **Aviation Fuel Interdiction:** International sanctions targeting Jet A-1 shipments have forced the junta to blend lower-grade domestic condensate and restrict training flights to conserve kerosene for combat sorties.
2. **Turbine Component Sanctions:** Russian (Klimov, Saturn) and Chinese engine overhauls require hard foreign currency and specialized air cargo shipments, creating extensive backlogs for grounded MiG-29 and JF-17 airframes.
3. **Pilot Attrition:** Extreme fatigue from flying 3 to 5 sorties per day per operational pilot, resulting in heightened navigation errors, friendly fire incidents, and controlled-flight-into-terrain (CFIT) accidents in mountainous terrain.
