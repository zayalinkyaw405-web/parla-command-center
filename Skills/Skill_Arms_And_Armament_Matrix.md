## 🏛️ 1. State Administration Council (SAC / Tatmadaw) Arsenal Profile

### A. Domestic Arms Manufacturing: KaPaSa (Directorate of Defence Industries - DDI)
The military regime relies on a network of over 25 specialized defence industrial factories (**KaPaSa** / ကာကွယ်ရေးပစ္စည်းစက်ရုံ):
- **Small Arms:**
  - `MA-1 Mk I/II`: Standard issue 5.56x45mm assault rifle (indigenous Galil derivative, modified polymer furniture).
  - `MA-2`: 5.56x45mm light machine gun with heavy barrel and bipod.
  - `MA-3`: 5.56x45mm compact carbine with folding buttstock.
  - `MA-4`: 5.56x45mm assault rifle integrated with indigenous 40mm underbarrel grenade launcher.
  - `MA-11`: 5.56x45mm licensed HK33/Galil hybrid variant.
  - `MA-15`: 7.62x51mm NATO general-purpose machine gun (MG3 derivative).
  - `MA-SNPR`: 7.62x51mm designated marksman/sniper rifle.
- **Heavy Mortars & Artillery:**
  - `MA-8 (60mm)`: Commando and infantry battalion mortar.
  - `MA-9 (81mm)`: Company fire-support mortar.
  - `MA-6 (120mm)`: Heavy battalion mortar (max range ~6.2 km).
  - `MAM-01 (122mm)`: 40-tube truck-mounted Multiple Launch Rocket System (Type 81 / BM-21 Grad derivative).
  - `MAM-02 (240mm)`: Heavy truck-mounted multiple launch rocket system for long-range saturation bombardment.
  - `D-30 / Type 59-1 (122mm / 130mm)`: Towed field howitzers manufactured under technology transfer agreements.
- **Aerial Munitions:**
  - 100-lb, 250-lb, and 500-lb unguided general-purpose iron bombs.
  - Fuel-Air Explosive (FAE / Thermobaric) vacuum munitions (e.g. deployed at Pa Zi Gyi).
  - 57mm S-5 and 80mm S-8 unguided aerial rocket pods.
- **Landmines:**
  - `MM-1`: Bounding antipersonnel fragmentation mine.
  - `MM-2`: Blast antipersonnel mine (plastic cased, low metallic signature).

### B. Imported Heavy Systems & Combat Aviation
- **Fixed-Wing Combat Aviation:**
  - `Sukhoi Su-30SME`: Multi-role twin-engine super-maneuverable air superiority fighter (Russian origin).
  - `MiG-29B / MiG-29SE`: Multi-role fighter (Russian origin).
  - `Yak-130`: Advanced jet trainer / light attack strike aircraft (high-density use in close air support).
  - `K-8 Karakorum / FTC-2000G`: Light strike/attack aircraft (Chinese / Pakistani origin).
- **Rotary Combat Aviation:**
  - `Mil Mi-35 Hind`: Dedicated heavy attack helicopter gunship (30mm twin autocannon, S-8 rockets).
  - `Mil Mi-17 Hip`: Heavy transport adapted with door gunners and external rocket pylons.
- **Armor & Mechanized Infantry:**
  - `T-72S`: Main battle tank (125mm smoothbore gun).
  - `Type 59D / Type 69-II`: Medium battle tanks.
  - `BTR-3U Guardian`: 8x8 wheeled amphibious armored personnel carrier with 30mm autocannon (Ukrainian license).
  - `Type 92 (WZ-551)`: 6x6 wheeled armored personnel carrier (Chinese origin).
  - `EE-9 Cascavel`: 6x6 armored reconnaissance vehicle armed with 90mm cannon (Brazilian origin).
- **Air Defense:**
  - `Pechora-2M (SA-3)`: Medium-range surface-to-air missile.
  - `KS-1M (HQ-12)`: Medium-to-long range SAM system.
  - `Igla (SA-16 / SA-18)`: Man-portable air defense missile.

## 🛡️ 2. Ethnic Armed Organizations (EAO) Arsenals

### A. United Wa State Army (UWSA - Panghsang)
- **Status:** De facto state army (~30,000–35,000 troops), state-tier military hardware.
- **Standard Rifles:** Type 81-Wa, QBZ-95 (5.8x42mm), Type 85 sniper rifle.
- **Heavy Firepower:** Type 90 122mm 40-tube MLRS, 122mm towed howitzers (Type 86), 105mm mountain guns, 120mm mortars.
- **Anti-Air & Anti-Armor:** FN-6 3rd-generation infrared homing MANPADS, HN-5 MANPADS, 14.5mm ZPU-4 four-barrel AAA, HJ-8 anti-tank guided missiles (ATGM).
- **Supply Role:** Acts as the primary arms intermediary, ammunition supplier, and technical broker for allied EAOs (MNDAA, TNLA, AA).

### B. Three Brotherhood Alliance (3BA)
- **MNDAA (Kokang) & TNLA (Ta'ang):**
  - *Small Arms:* Type 81-1, Type 56-1, Type 81 LMG (7.62x39mm), M16 variants.
  - *The Drone Revolution (Operation 1027):* Pioneer of massed tactical drone strikes in Myanmar. Deploy heavy agricultural hexacopters and custom octocopters carrying 4 to 8 stabilized 60mm/81mm mortar bombs or shaped-charge HEAT warheads; widespread use of high-speed FPV (First Person View) racing suicide drones targeting bunker embrasures and artillery emplacements.
  - *Heavy Weapons:* Captured SAC 122mm D-30 howitzers, 120mm MA-6 mortars, captured BTR-3U and Type 92 APCs.
- **Arakan Army (AA - Western Command):**
  - *Small Arms:* Type 81, Type 56, customized sniper platforms, 40mm grenade launchers.
  - *Siege Artillery:* Highly accurate 120mm heavy mortar units used to break fortified junta hilltops; captured naval patrol gunboats refitted for coastal and riverine operations.
  - *Air Defense:* FN-6 and HN-5 MANPADS; pickup-mounted 14.5mm twin anti-aircraft guns.

### C. Kachin Independence Army (KIA - Northern Command)
- **Indigenous Weapons Factories (KIO Technical Department):**
  - `K-09`: Indigenous Kachin assault rifle (7.62x39mm) built on the Type 81 operating mechanism.
  - `K-10`: Modernized carbine variant with modular rail system and synthetic furniture.
  - `Indigenous Mortars`: 60mm and 81mm mortar tubes, bipods, cast iron bomb casings, and point-detonating fuzes.
- **Air Defense:** FN-6 MANPADS (responsible for multiple downs of SAC fighter jets and attack helicopters), 12.7mm DShK / Type 54 heavy machine guns, 14.5mm ZPU-1/2.

### D. KNU / KNLA, KNDF, and People's Defence Forces (PDFs)
- **Infantry Small Arms:** High heterogeneity from Thai black market and decentralized channels:
  - Western 5.56x45mm: M16A1, M4A1 carbines, Colt AR-15, Steyr AUG, HK33.
  - 3D-Printed Arms: **FGC-9 Mk II** (9x19mm Parabellum semi-automatic pistol-caliber carbine), printed extensively by anti-coup resistance groups using consumer 3D printers and electrochemical rifling.
  - Captured KaPaSa Weapons: MA-1, MA-2, MA-3, MA-4.
- **Drone Weaponization:**
  - Hundreds of decentralized FPV strike teams operating 7-inch to 10-inch quadcopters carrying 1.5–2.5 kg of explosive payload (TNT/C4, modified PG-7V warheads) detonated on contact with copper wires.

## 🔐 5. Zero-Trust Ingress & Arms Attribution Protocol

When logging battlefield weapons telemetry into Parla's `OfflineLedger`:
1. **Domain:** `domain="arms_telemetry"`.
2. **Attribution Verification:** Validate spent casing headstamp or recovered serial code against KaPaSa factory indices or Chinese export lot numbers.
3. **Caliber Normalization:** Ensure metric caliber normalization (`5.56x45mm`, `7.62x39mm`, etc.).
4. **Zero-Trust Sanitization:** Mask unit designations, operator callsigns, and GPS coordinates coarsened to 2 decimal places (~1.1 km).
5. **Ledger Sealing:** Cryptographically commit payload into `Data/parla_ledger.db`.
