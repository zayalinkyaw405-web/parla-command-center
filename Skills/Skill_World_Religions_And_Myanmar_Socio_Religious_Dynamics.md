### 3.1 The "Light" Axis: Compassion, Sanctuary & Peaceful Resistance
1. **The Saffron Tradition of Resistance**:
   - Monks in Myanmar have historically stood at the vanguard of anti-colonial and anti-authoritarian struggles (U Ottama, U Wisara).
   - In the 2007 Saffron Revolution and following the 2021 coup, thousands of patriotic monks performed *Patam Nikkujjana Kamma* (inverting alms bowls), spiritually excommunicating the military junta by refusing alms.
   - Monasteries across the Bamar heartland (Sagaing, Magway, Mandalay) serve as decentralized humanitarian networks, covert food storehouses, and underground schools for children whose state schools were closed or bombed.
2. **Christian Highland Networks as De Facto Civil Society**:
   - In Chin State, Kachin State, and Karenni (Kayah), local church associations (such as the Kachin Baptist Convention, Chin Baptist Convention, Karen Baptist Convention, and Roman Catholic Dioceses) represent the primary functioning social infrastructure.
   - Church compounds provide shelter, emergency triage, international liaison, and distribution of rice and medicine to over 800,000 ethnic minority IDPs.
3. **Inter-Faith Solidarity in the Spring Revolution**:
   - Unlike previous decades where the military successfully sparked communal riots (e.g., 2012–2014 anti-Muslim pogroms), the post-2021 revolutionary movement has witnessed unprecedented inter-faith solidarity: Buddhist, Muslim, Christian, and Hindu youth protesting together and forming joint local defense formations.

### 3.2 The "Dark" Axis: State Co-optation, Extremism & Sacrilege
1. **Ethno-Religious Majoritarian Extremism (*MaBaTha* & 969)**:
   - Formed under the leadership of radical monks like U Wirathu, the Association for Protection of Race and Religion (*MaBaTha*) weaponized Buddhist symbols to spread anti-Muslim hysteria and incite pogroms against the Rohingya in Rakhine and Muslims across central Myanmar.
   - Following the 2021 coup, the SAC pardoned and decorated Wirathu, utilizing radical monks to organize *Pyusawhti* pro-military death squads in Sagaing (e.g., monk U Wasawa distributing firearms to villagers).
2. **Junta Weaponization of Sacred Monuments (*Maravijaya*)**:
   - Military rulers seek to emulate ancient Burmese monarchs (*Chakkavatti* - Universal Monarchs) by building colossal religious monuments to cleanse bad karma (*Kamma*).
   - Min Aung Hlaing erected the *Maravijaya* colossus in Naypyidaw—the world's tallest marble Buddha statue (81 feet, 1,780 tonnes)—as a grand ideological monument to legitimise his regime and secure monastic subservience.
3. **Deliberate Sacrilege & Desecration as Counter-Insurgency Doctrine**:
   - Operating under the scorched-earth *Four Cuts* doctrine, the SAC systematically bombs and shells religious structures suspected of sheltering civilians or resistance fighters:
     - **Churches**: Over 120 Christian churches and parish buildings destroyed in Chin State (Thantlang burned), Kayah State (Sacred Heart Church, Loikaw Cathedral compound), and Kachin State.
     - **Monasteries**: Over 180 Buddhist monasteries torched or airstruck in Sagaing and Magway Regions, with resident monks executed for harboring displaced villagers.
     - **Mosques**: Dozens of mosques demolished in northern Rakhine and Sagaing, preventing displaced Muslim communities from returning.
4. **Military Occultism & *Yadaya* Rituals**:
   - Myanmar military commanders routinely consult court astrologers (*Beda*) to schedule military operations and conduct *Yadaya* rituals designed to neutralize threats:
     - Dressing in female garments (*Htamain*) to ritually neutralize female political authority (Aung San Suu Kyi).
     - Altering national currency denominations to multiples of 9 (Ne Win's 45 and 90-kyat banknotes).
     - Symbolic sacrificial offerings at specific astrological hours to deflect defeat.

## 4. International Humanitarian Law (IHL) & Protected Sacred Sites
Under customary International Humanitarian Law (Article 53 of Additional Protocol I and Article 16 of Additional Protocol II to the Geneva Conventions), **places of worship and historic monuments are protected property**. 

Attacks against religious buildings constitute war crimes unless they are being used for military purposes (and even then, strict proportionality and warning rules apply).

Parla's religious incident telemetry classifies damage into four strict severities:
- `CRITICAL_TOTAL_DESTRUCTION`: Complete structural demolition, airstrike direct hit, or intentional arson.
- `MAJOR_STRUCTURAL_DAMAGE`: Heavy artillery shelling penetration, roof collapse, stained glass/shrine destruction.
- `MODERATE_DESECRATION`: Ground troops looting religious artifacts, billeting inside sanctums, bullet pockmarks.
- `THREAT_INTIMIDATION`: Aerial flyover buzzing, surrounding artillery fire, intimidation of clergy.
