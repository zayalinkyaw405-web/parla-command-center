## 🏔️ Strategic Mineral Belts & Geological Formations

### 1. Pangwa-Chipwi Heavy Rare Earths (HREO)
- **Geographic Setting:** Kachin Special Region 1 (N'Mai Hka watershed, China border).
- **Host Rocks:** Highly weathered granitic regolith (Ion-Adsorption Clays).
- **Target Elements:** Dysprosium (Dy > 150 ppm), Terbium (Tb > 40 ppm), Neodymium (Nd), Praseodymium (Pr).
- **Extraction Mechanism:** In-situ chemical leaching using ammonium sulfate injection ponds.
- **Key Hazards:** Groundwater acidification (pH < 4.5), nitrogenous ammonium runoff, slope destabilization.
- **Controlling Actor:** Kachin Independence Army (KIA / KIO).

### 2. Hpakant-Tawmaw Jadeite Tract
- **Geographic Setting:** Kachin State (Uru River Basin).
- **Host Rocks:** Serpentinite-hosted jadeitite dikes within the Sagaing Fault / Indo-Burman ophiolite zone.
- **Target Minerals:** High-grade pyroxene jadeite (NaAlSi2O6), omphacite.
- **Extraction Mechanism:** Heavy mechanical open-pit strip mining and artisanal hand scavenging (Yemase).
- **Key Hazards:** Massive monsoon dump-waste landslides (displacement rate > 120 mm/yr), open-pit waterlogged collapse.
- **Controlling Actor:** KIA & Allied Mining Cooperatives.

### 3. Mogok Metamorphic Belt (MMB)
- **Geographic Setting:** Mandalay Region & Shan State frontier.
- **Host Rocks:** Marble-hosted skarns, gneiss, and granitic pegmatites.
- **Target Gems:** Pigeon's blood rubies, royal blue sapphires, spinel, peridot.
- **Extraction Mechanism:** Hard-rock deep tunneling and alluvial gravel washing.
- **Key Hazards:** Artisanal tunnel cave-ins, unventilated gas pockets, uncontrolled blasting.
- **Controlling Actor:** Ta'ang National Liberation Army (TNLA) & MDY-PDF.

### 4. Mawchi Tin-Tungsten Lode Complex
- **Geographic Setting:** Kayah (Karenni) State (Kemapyu watershed).
- **Host Rocks:** Hydrothermal quartz-cassiterite-wolframite veins cutting Mesozoic granites.
- **Target Minerals:** Cassiterite (SnO2, Sn > 0.8%), Wolframite ((Fe,Mn)WO4, W > 0.5%).
- **Extraction Mechanism:** Underground adit mining and gravity separation sluicing.
- **Key Hazards:** Heavy metal and arsenic tailings erosion into Salween River tributaries.
- **Controlling Actor:** Karenni Nationalities Defence Force (KNDF / IEC).

### 5. Monywa / Letpadaung Porphyry Copper Complex
- **Geographic Setting:** Sagaing Region (Central Volcanic Arc).
- **Host Rocks:** High-sulfidation epithermal/porphyry copper volcanic intrusive.
- **Target Commodities:** Copper Cathodes (Cu > 0.6%, 99.99% refined).
- **Extraction Mechanism:** Open pit with sulfuric acid heap leaching and SX-EW processing.
- **Key Hazards:** Sulfuric acid pregnant leach solution leakage, airborne copper dust.
- **Controlling Actor:** SAC Military / Myanmar Wanbao Mining Joint Venture.

## 🔬 Sensor Standards & Calibration Thresholds

### 1. Triaxial Geophone Monitoring (In-Situ)
- **Bandwidth:** 10 Hz – 500 Hz.
- **Dynamic Range:** > 120 dB.
- **Rockburst / Blasting Discrimination:**
  - *Controlled Blasting:* Discrete impulse waveform, high P-wave to S-wave ratio, duration < 1.5 s.
  - *Structural Slope Creep:* Continuous low-frequency microseismic rumbling (15–45 Hz), duration > 10 s.
  - *Tectonic Rupture:* Broad-spectrum low-frequency shear wave arrival (0.5–20 Hz).

### 2. Field Portable XRF (pXRF) Spectrometry
- **Excitation Source:** Ag/Rh anode X-ray tube (40–50 kV, 100–200 µA).
- **Minimum Detection Limits (LOD):**
  - Copper (Cu): 10 ppm
  - Tin (Sn): 20 ppm
  - Tungsten (W): 15 ppm
  - Dysprosium (Dy): 10 ppm
  - Terbium (Tb): 5 ppm
- **Calibration Protocol:** Certified Reference Material (CRM) standard disc run every 20 field shots.

### 3. Satellite InSAR Ground Deformation Tracking
- **Sensor Feeds:** Sentinel-1 C-band SAR (12-day repeat cycle) & ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 L-band.
- **Displacement Velocity Thresholds:**
  - *Stable Ground:* < 15 mm/yr
  - *Active Settling:* 15 – 45 mm/yr (elevated monitoring)
  - *Critical Slope Failure Risk:* > 80 mm/yr (immediate pit-wall evacuation alarm)

## 🔐 Zero-Trust Ingress & Ledgering Schema

When ingesting geological telemetry into Parla's `OfflineLedger`:
1. **Domain:** `domain="geological_telemetry"`.
2. **GPS Coarsening:** Exact coordinates coarsened to 2 decimal places (~1.1 km grid cell) to safeguard local field monitors and artisanal miners.
3. **PII Masking:** Operator names, concession holder IDs, and vehicle registrations are irreversibly stripped.
4. **Integrity Sealing:** Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature chained into `Data/parla_ledger.db`.
5. **Fail-Safe Offline Mode:** In the event of network disruption, records are buffered locally in SQLite WAL mode.
