1. Executive Summary

Between January 2022 and October 2026, the Myanmar civil conflict escalated into one of the world's most acute remote violence theaters. Facing widespread ground territorial losses to Ethnic Armed Organizations (EAOs) and People's Defence Forces (PDFs) following the 2021 coup, the military junta (State Administration Council - SAC) weaponized combat aviation as its principal offensive and retaliatory instrument.

Conventional conflict observation architectures suffer from severe structural latency (4 to 14 days) and depend on telecommunications infrastructure that is deliberately cut by the military prior to air operations. This report demonstrates that integrating autonomous, low-power Acoustic Edge Sensors, NASA FIRMS satellite thermal streams, and decentralized LoRa/Meshtastic mesh networks bridges the data gap, delivering 4 to 8 minutes of pre-impact warning to vulnerable civilian populations while providing immutable, tamper-evident conflict telemetry for international accountability bodies.

3. Intersection of IoT Agentic AI & Conflict Telemetry

Autonomous intelligence platforms operated in conflict environments must resolve the core trade-off between physical observation constraints (Yin), high-entropy environmental destruction (Chaos), and actionable decision-making (Yang).

A. Edge Micro-Doppler Acoustic Inference (Yin)

Fixed-wing combat aircraft deployed in Myanmar (e.g., K-8, FTC-2000G, Su-30) produce distinct acoustic emissions between 1 kHz and 8 kHz generated by compressor blades and exhaust velocity. Low-power edge nodes (powered by ARM Cortex-M4/M33 microcontrollers with 10W solar collectors) running TinyML neural networks achieve continuous passive surveillance with sub-1.5W power draw, identifying jet turbine ingress 8 to 12 kilometers out.

B. Decentralized Sub-Gigahertz Mesh Backbones (The Chaos Bridge)

By avoiding cellular dependence, acoustic nodes transmit alert tokens via 868 / 915 MHz LoRa mesh radio (Meshtastic protocol) across 15–25 km hops. These encrypted packets directly actuate physical village warning sirens and battery-powered LED beacons, granting communities 4 to 8 minutes to reach shelter trenches before ordnance impact.

4. IoT Agent Optimization & Red Team Compliance Plan

Red Team Principle: PII Sanitization & Physical Safety
In an asymmetric conflict, telemetry collection carries lethal risk for local populations. Parla enforces zero-PII retention on edge devices. All network telemetry, operator IDs, IP addresses, and GPS coordinates of human observers are cryptographically masked or spatial-jittered prior to persistence.

Strategic Implementation Roadmap:

  1. Stage 1 — Multi-Sensor Edge Pilot (Months 1–3): Deploy 25 solar-powered LoRa acoustic sensor nodes across pilot civilian corridors in Sagaing Region in coordination with humanitarian civil defense teams.
