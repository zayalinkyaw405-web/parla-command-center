### 2026-03-20
- **Topic:** SAC Command Restructuring & Transition to Coordinated Strike Packages
- **Summary:** ACLED and defense analysts document a doctrinal shift by the Myanmar Air Force: rather than isolated single-aircraft sorties, the military began utilizing 2 to 5 synchronized jet fighters (primarily K-8 Karakorum and FTC-2000G light combat jets) to conduct sequential bombing passes over designated resistance-administered targets.
- **Source Links:**
  - ACLED Conflict Analysis: [https://acleddata.com/](https://acleddata.com/)

### 2025-12-15
- **Topic:** UN OHCHR Declares 2025 Deadliest Year for Aerial Civilian Casualties
- **Summary:** UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk published verification of over 702 civilian deaths in a 6-month evaluation window, with airstrikes accounting for 57% of all fatalities. The report noted a 50%+ year-on-year increase in aerial missions compared to 2024, driven by increased deployment of weaponized reconnaissance drones alongside fixed-wing aircraft.
- **Source Links:**
  - UN OHCHR Myanmar Situation Report: [https://www.ohchr.org/en/countries/myanmar](https://www.ohchr.org/en/countries/myanmar)

### 2025-06-18
- **Topic:** Humanitarian IoT & Acoustic Early Warning System Precedents in Conflict Zones
- **Summary:** Academic and defense reviews highlight the successful deployment of acoustic sensor meshes (such as Hala Systems' Sentry in Syria and Ukraine) which achieve 7–10 minute civilian warning windows. Humanitarian engineers propose adapting similar micro-Doppler edge acoustic arrays across resistance-controlled Myanmar via LoRaWAN and Meshtastic RF networks.
- **Source Links:**
  - Hala Systems Humanitarian Sentry Review: [https://halasystems.com/](https://halasystems.com/)
  - MIT Technology Review on Conflict AI: [https://www.technologyreview.com/](https://www.technologyreview.com/)

### 2024-11-05
- **Topic:** Fall of Lashio & Escalation of Retaliatory Infrastructure Bombing
- **Summary:** Following the capture of the Northeastern Military Command in Lashio by resistance forces during Operation 1027 Phase II, junta forces initiated sustained aerial bombardment of civilian neighborhoods, municipal water supplies, and hospitals inside captured urban centers to deny civic governance.
- **Source Links:**
  - ACLED Special Report on Operation 1027: [https://acleddata.com/](https://acleddata.com/)
  - The Irrawaddy Conflict Monitor: [https://www.irrawaddy.com/](https://www.irrawaddy.com/)

### 2023-04-11
- **Topic:** The Pa Zi Gyi Thermobaric Munition Strike (Kanbalu, Sagaing)
- **Summary:** In the single deadliest aerial attack of the conflict, a military Su-30/Yak-130 strike delivered thermobaric and cluster munitions onto a gathering in Pa Zi Gyi village, killing 168+ civilians. The incident triggered urgent international calls for aviation fuel embargoes and the deployment of civilian acoustic detection networks.
- **Source Links:**
  - Human Rights Watch Report on Pa Zi Gyi: [https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/05/09/myanmar-military-airstrike-used-thermobaric-weapon](https://www.hrw.org/news/2023/05/09/myanmar-military-airstrike-used-thermobaric-weapon)

### 2022-10-23
- **Topic:** A Nang Pa Anniversary Concert Bombing (Hpakant, Kachin)
- **Summary:** Three Yak-130 jet fighters dropped multiple unguided high-explosive bombs on a public outdoor gathering, killing over 80 people. This event marked the systematic normalization of using combat jets against dense, non-combatant public crowds in non-regime controlled territory.
